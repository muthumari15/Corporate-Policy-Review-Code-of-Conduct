# Corporate Policy Review -Code of Conduct



This project is a case study on the Employee Code of Conduct of Wipro Limited. It explains the importance of honesty, integrity, respect, confidentiality, and professional behaviour in the workplace.

It also covers company rules, corporate governance, policy evaluation, business relevance, and recommendations for improving ethical practices.

**Objective:** To understand how a Code of Conduct helps employees follow ethical rules, protect company information, and build trust in the workplace.

**Company Studied:** Wipro Limited
